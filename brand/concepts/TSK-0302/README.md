# TSK-0302 — Provisional Visual Identity Direction Board

**Task:** TSK-0302 — Develop and evaluate a small set of coherent visual identity directions  
**Acceptance:** ACC-0302  
**Lifecycle:** L4 — Product Definition, Requirements & Experience Design  
**Action authority:** A3 / AUTO_ALLOWED  
**Status:** PROVISIONAL INTERNAL CONCEPT EXPLORATION / NOT FINAL IDENTITY  
**Date:** 2026-08-29

## 1. Authority and scope boundary

This board explores **three** deliberately small visual directions for later Project Owner selection under HUMAN_ONLY `TSK-0301`. It does not select a final logo, typography system, palette, imagery system, iconography system, layout system, public brand package or implementation tokens.

The directions inherit:

- `TSK-0298` — calm, clear, privacy-respecting, non-surveillance First Phone Safety Setup strategy; truthful limits; safer independence rather than total control;
- `TSK-0299` — plain-language, non-alarmist, child-aware verbal system and evidence-matched protection language;
- `REQ-0024` — eventual full brand system completeness;
- `REQ-0025` — full polish must not outrun sufficient proposition/behavioral evidence;
- `CON-0017` — multilingual/RTL capability without implying non-UK market activation;
- `CON-0023` — correctness, security/privacy, reliability/trust and quality outrank autonomy/speed/cost;
- `DEC-0051 / CR-0004` — bounded internal visual exploration is allowed while representative-parent validation remains deferred.

`RSK-0002` remains OPEN. No direction is called parent-preferred, trustworthy to parents, behaviorally validated, legally approved, launch-ready or implementation-ready merely because it is visually coherent.

## 2. Hard visual constraints

Every direction must:

1. remain recognizable as a simple abstract mark without depending on text;
2. be editable vector source with no embedded raster assets;
3. scale without fixed-resolution detail;
4. avoid surveillance motifs: eyes, cameras, target reticles, tracking maps, location pins used as monitoring symbols;
5. avoid militarized or absolute-security symbolism such as a closed impenetrable shield/fortress paired with protection claims;
6. avoid childish/toy styling that undermines the intended parent-facing context;
7. avoid certification badges, stars, medals or visual authority theatre;
8. avoid a single overall green/check/safety-score visual that could imply every protection layer is verified;
9. remain reducible to one colour later without losing the core silhouette;
10. allow RTL layouts later because the mark itself contains no directional wordmark or language-specific glyph;
11. use accent colour only as identity expression, never as the sole carrier of protection state;
12. preserve generous geometry suitable for small/mobile display and favicons after later owner refinement.

## 3. Direction A — Open Path

**Master:** `direction-a-open-path.svg`

### Concept

Three rounded horizontal guardrail segments progress toward one open endpoint. The three lines loosely correspond to Phone / Internet / Service while the opening communicates a guided path rather than enclosure or surveillance.

### Strategic fit

- **Clear:** minimal geometry and immediate directional progression.
- **Practical:** looks like a sequence/path rather than an abstract cyber-security emblem.
- **Non-controlling:** deliberately open, not enclosed.
- **Three-layer compatible:** supports the current Phone / Internet / Service product structure without pretending all three are technically the same safeguard.

### Provisional palette

- Deep blue: `#12344D` — primary dark identity colour.
- Teal: `#147D64` — secondary identity colour.
- Burnt orange: `#C75B12` — small accent only.
- White/off-white backgrounds are preferred for evaluation.

Colour is not state semantics. `Verified`, `Action needed`, `Not covered`, `Status uncertain`, etc. remain separately owned by the product state system.

### Strengths

- strongest literal relationship to a guided setup journey;
- simple geometry with three visible layers;
- no shield/camera/lock/checkmark dependency;
- very low asset complexity.

### Risks / owner questions for TSK-0301

- may feel more like navigation/process than a standalone trust brand;
- three horizontal bars can become generic if the later wordmark/system does not add distinctiveness;
- orange endpoint must remain an identity accent, not be misread as a warning/status dot.

## 4. Direction B — Open Guardrails

**Master:** `direction-b-open-guardrails.svg`

### Concept

Two open rounded rails guide toward a forward point. The form expresses **guardrails + independence** without closing into a shield. It is intentionally asymmetric and open.

### Strategic fit

- **Protective, not controlling:** rails guide rather than enclose.
- **Safer independence:** the forward point suggests movement/choice.
- **Calm:** curved geometry avoids aggressive cyber-security language.
- **Truthful:** no tick/check/lock/100%-coverage symbol.

### Provisional palette

- Charcoal: `#1F2937` — primary dark identity colour.
- Blue: `#2563EB` — secondary identity colour.
- Warm brown/orange: `#B45309` — accent only.

### Strengths

- strongest visual expression of the accepted “guardrails for safer independence” brand idea;
- open silhouette makes the non-enclosure principle visible;
- remains simple enough for mono and small-size refinement.

### Risks / owner questions for TSK-0301

- forward triangle can read as a media/play/navigation icon if not refined carefully;
- guardrail metaphor is conceptually strong but less directly tied to the three product layers;
- asymmetric form requires later optical balancing with the wordmark.

## 5. Direction C — Connected Layers

**Master:** `direction-c-connected-layers.svg`

### Concept

Three offset open rounded frames overlap lightly. Each frame remains distinct while touching the others, representing Phone / Internet / Service as separate but connected safeguard layers.

### Strategic fit

- **One coherent path, distinct controls:** emphasizes coordination without collapsing layers into one system-verification claim.
- **Truth before apparent completion:** open frames avoid a single total-protection enclosure.
- **Scalable system thinking:** geometry can later inform component framing, section containers or icon construction if the owner selects it.

### Provisional palette

- Deep green: `#12372A` — primary dark identity colour.
- Indigo: `#4338CA` — secondary identity colour.
- Warm brown/orange: `#B45309` — accent only.

### Strengths

- strongest link to the current three-layer product model;
- visually distinct from common shield/lock parental-safety marks;
- offers the richest potential for a later modular design language without requiring that expansion now.

### Risks / owner questions for TSK-0301

- overlapping frames are the most complex of the three and need small-size optical testing before finalization;
- could drift toward generic “platform/system” branding if later typography/wordmark is too technical;
- relationship between the three frames must not imply equal verification across all layers.

## 7. Accessibility and colour-use rules

These concept palettes are **identity-direction palettes**, not the final accessibility token system.

Mandatory later rules if any direction is selected:

- ordinary text must meet WCAG 2.2 AA contrast against its actual background;
- use the dark primary colours for body/critical text only after exact contrast verification;
- secondary/accent colours are not automatically approved for small text;
- protection states may never depend on brand colour alone;
- status label + text meaning remain mandatory under TSK-0320/TSK-0314;
- focus/error/success/status tokens are defined separately in the later design-system task rather than inferred from these identity colours;
- no logo mark carries necessary UI/status information.

The automated TSK-0302 verification checks the dark/secondary candidate colours against white and records exact WCAG contrast ratios. Any colour below 4.5:1 against white is treated as decorative/large-mark-only, not small-text-approved.

## 8. Editability and production restraint

Each master is human-readable SVG with:

- a `256 × 256` viewBox;
- only basic `<path>` / `<circle>` geometry;
- no embedded bitmap;
- no font dependency;
- no JavaScript;
- no external resources;
- no filters/masks required for identity recognition.

This is intentionally the complete TSK-0302 asset set. Wordmarks, typography masters, full palettes, icon families, templates, social assets, stationery, favicons, app icons and production tokens are **not** generated here because that would prematurely perform TSK-0301/TSK-0300 or later brand-package work.

After TSK-0302 is independently verified and reconciled PASS, the owner should choose one of these dispositions under HUMAN_ONLY TSK-0301:

- **Select A — Open Path** for refinement into the final identity system;
- **Select B — Open Guardrails** for refinement into the final identity system;
- **Select C — Connected Layers** for refinement into the final identity system;

Selecting a direction later does not make behavioral validation true. Final identity masters must still satisfy TSK-0301 small/mobile/mono/contrast/readability acceptance and preserve all current evidence/claims boundaries.

1. exactly a small set of three directions exists;

3. all are editable/scalable SVG without raster/font/external dependency;
4. all preserve the strategy and avoid surveillance/absolute-safety symbolism;
5. accessibility/contrast constraints are explicit and testable;
