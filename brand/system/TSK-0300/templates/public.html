How it works

SafeWeb brings together the applicable phone safeguard, the supported encrypted-DNS internet baseline, and at most one relevant service safeguard. Each layer keeps its own evidence state and limitations.

Compatibility & limits

SafeWeb does not make a complete-safety promise. Support and verification depend on the current device, platform, network and safeguard mechanism.

Privacy by restraint

The core setup remains accountless-first. Optional parent-account continuity may be used to manage minimum device records, but sign-in does not automatically import anonymous setup state and SafeWeb does not make browsing or DNS-query history a product feature.

Help

Use source-backed self-service guidance for setup, verification, removal and recovery.

Optional account continuity

Sign in only if you want bounded account/session continuity and lightweight device management. Account ownership does not prove that a device is currently protected, and the complete core setup remains available without an account.
