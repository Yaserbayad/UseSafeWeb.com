Clear guardrails for safer first-phone independence.

SafeWeb helps you put sensible first-phone safeguards in place through one clear, privacy-respecting setup path — and tells you plainly what is protected, what still needs attention, and what is not covered.

How it works

SafeWeb brings together the applicable phone safeguard, the supported encrypted-DNS internet baseline, and at most one relevant service safeguard. Each layer keeps its own evidence state and limitations.

Compatibility & limits

SafeWeb does not make a complete-safety promise. Support and verification depend on the current device, platform, network and safeguard mechanism.

Privacy by restraint

The active baseline is accountless-first and does not make browsing or DNS-query history a product feature.
