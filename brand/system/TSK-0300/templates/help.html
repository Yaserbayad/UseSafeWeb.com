Help & recovery

Fix the current issue without guessing

Use the smallest relevant source-backed check. Do not repeat the same failed action unless the condition has changed.

1. Identify the current state

If SafeWeb cannot determine the effective protection path, keep the state uncertain rather than showing a positive result.

2. Apply one known correction

Follow the approved device/network instruction for the specific cause. Do not collect browsing history or unrestricted diagnostics to force certainty.

3. Recheck or remove

Recheck after the relevant condition changes. If needed, use the platform-specific removal path and confirm normal connectivity recovery separately from protection.

Self-service is the normal support model. Exceptional owner intervention is reserved for unusual technical, legal, security or safeguarding incidents; this template does not promise routine staffed live support.
