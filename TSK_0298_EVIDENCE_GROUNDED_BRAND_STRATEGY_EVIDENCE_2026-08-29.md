### B. Non-surveillance and claims constraints — PASS

`PROTECTION_CLAIMS_CHECKLIST.md` — blob `4bfc83421318fe761d06f9a63e052e3bff36070a` — requires evidence-matched protection states, explicit DNS limitations, no complete-safety wording, no surveillance implication, current-evidence verification and clear removal/recovery.

TSK-0298 binds those controls into both a positive/conditional claim matrix and an explicit prohibited-expression register. It expressly prohibits:

- complete/100% safety or “blocks everything” claims;
- child browsing/activity monitoring or covert-surveillance framing;
- false “Protected/verified” states;
- bypass-proof/permanent-protection claims;
- unsupported absolute privacy language such as “zero data”;
- unsupported legal/compliance certification;
- behavioral-validation claims such as “parents love it” or “proven easy”;
- unsupported “best/only/unique” comparative claims;
- fear/shame pressure;
- false device/market/launch readiness;
- fabricated 24/7/routine human support;
- pay-for-better-safety/premium protection framing.

This is stricter than merely avoiding a small set of phrases: the strategy defines the evidence condition under which allowed/conditional claims may be used.
