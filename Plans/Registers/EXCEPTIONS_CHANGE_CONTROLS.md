# Deferred exceptions and change controls


| Exception | Capability/work | Disposition | Activation trigger | Current rule | Authority |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| EXC-0001 | Optional account/authentication/persistent parent dashboard | DEFERRED_EXCEPTION | Validated persistence, multi-device, recovery, supporter, or equivalent need; accountless alternatives inadequate; owner approval; privacy/security/architecture/UX gates pass. | No mandatory account/auth vendor/persistent identity/dashboard in active baseline. | Project Owner |
| EXC-0002 | Paid acquisition experiment | DEFERRED_EXCEPTION | Primary earned engine, observed economics and capacity justify a bounded test; explicit budget/audience/privacy/cap/stop approval. | No plan dependency on paid acquisition. | Project Owner |
| EXC-0003 | Full Turkish/Arabic localization | DEFERRED_EXCEPTION | Specific locale/market passes LG-16; product/legal/support/channel/content readiness complete. | Implement i18n/RTL readiness only; no premature full localization. | Project Owner |
| EXC-0004 | High-availability/multi-node architecture | DEFERRED_EXCEPTION | Measured outage impact, capacity, RTO failure, partner/SLA or risk evidence justifies complexity/cost. | Use single lean recoverable topology and approximately 30-minute recovery model. | Project Owner |
| EXC-0005 | GROW automation, native app, child account, school portal, broad DNS administration | DEFERRED_EXCEPTION | Validated material customer value, current product success, explicit scope decision, full safety/privacy/security/cost/support gates. | Exclude from current product and critical path. | Project Owner |
| EXC-0006 | Fundraising program | DEFERRED_EXCEPTION | New explicit owner strategic decision after approximately first two years or extraordinary material necessity. | No fundraising roadmap, tasks, or budget. | Project Owner |
| EXC-0007 | Official geographic/market expansion | DEFERRED_EXCEPTION | Named market passes LG-16; technical availability alone is insufficient. | Do not claim localized support beyond approved markets. | Project Owner |
| EXC-0008 | Routine staffed customer-support operation | DEFERRED_EXCEPTION | Evidence proves ordinary issues cannot be safely automated/productized and a funded owner-approved operating model is necessary. | Self-service and exceptional escalation remain baseline. | Project Owner |
| EXC-0009 | Institutional sponsorship/white-label/B2B2C model | DEFERRED_EXCEPTION | Consumer value, trust, economics, independence, privacy, partner fit and operational capacity are proven; owner approves. | Not required for validation or initial launch. | Project Owner |
| EXC-0010 | Modified/distributed/embedded AdGuard GPL integration | DEFERRED_EXCEPTION | Material technical need plus specialist license review and owner decision. | Use separate unmodified AdGuard process/API/config integration. | Project Owner |

Material change records use `CR-xxxx`; execution exceptions use `EXC-xxxx`. A change record must identify affected objectives/requirements/constraints/packages/interfaces/tasks/tests/evidence/gates/risks, owner authority, decision, implementation/migration/rollback, and canonical read-back.
