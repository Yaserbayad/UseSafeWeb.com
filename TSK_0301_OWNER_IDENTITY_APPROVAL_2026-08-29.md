# TSK-0301 — Project Owner Identity Approval

**Date:** 2026-08-29  
**Task:** TSK-0301  
**Authority:** Project Owner / HUMAN_ONLY selection authority  
**Decision:** APPROVED

## Approved identity

The Project Owner explicitly approved the final Concept A wordmark direction and authorized governed continuation.

The approved visible brand identity is:

- **Brand name:** `SafeWeb`
- **Primary wordmark:** `Safe` in dark forest green; `Web` in warm maroon.
- **Primary dark green:** `#173F35`.
- **Deep green:** `#0F2D23`.
- **Maroon:** `#7A2E36`.
- **Off-white:** `#F6F4EF`.
- **Muted sage:** `#A7BEAD`.
- **Primary style:** minimalist, wordmark-first, simple, clear, direct, memorable, calm, modern and trustworthy.
- **Visual construction:** preserve the approved Concept A typographic direction; no symbol-first primary logo.
- **Monogram/favicon:** `Sw` derived from the wordmark, dark-green field, light `S`, maroon `w` where a two-colour treatment is appropriate.
- **Light-surface treatment:** `Safe` dark green / `Web` maroon.
- **Dark-surface approved display treatment:** `Safe` off-white / `Web` maroon, subject to the accessibility fallback rules in the implementation specification.

## Brand/domain distinction

`SafeWeb` is the visible brand name. `UseSafeWeb.com` remains the project/domain/repository identity and may appear where the actual domain or technical project identifier is required. The `Use` prefix is **not** part of the visible brand wordmark and must not be reused as brand copy or logo text.

## Owner direction incorporated

The approval incorporates the owner's explicit requirements that the identity:

1. remain extremely close to the selected Concept A rather than being redesigned;
2. be minimalist and primarily the name itself;
3. use dark green as the core identity colour;
4. add warmth by using maroon materially in the logo/identity rather than merely listing it in a palette;
5. use maroon for the complete `Web` portion of the approved `SafeWeb` wordmark;
6. preserve simplicity, clarity, directness and memorability.

## Prohibited inference

This approval is a visual-identity decision only. It does not establish or imply:

- representative-parent preference or comprehension;
- behavioral validation;
- legal/privacy completion;
- participant activation authority;
- integrated product build authority beyond separately eligible WBS work;
- publication, payment, market-readiness or launch authority.

`RSK-0002` remains OPEN and `TSK-0187` remains the mandatory future representative-parent behavioral-validation dependency where the WBS requires it.

## Stable owner disposition

**TSK-0301 identity selection: OWNER APPROVED — SafeWeb Concept A, wordmark-first, `Safe` dark green / `Web` maroon.**
