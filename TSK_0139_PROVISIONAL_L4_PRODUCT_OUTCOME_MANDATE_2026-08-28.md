## 3. Provisional job assumption

The current **provisional, unvalidated** job assumption is:

> Help a parent/caregiver around a child's first independently used smartphone establish sensible safeguards across the phone, internet baseline and one relevant service without forcing the parent to understand several disconnected systems or resort to invasive monitoring.

This is grounded in the owner-frozen product/job decisions and the synthetic operating model. It is **not** described as a validated customer job until real behavioral evidence exists.

## 4. Provisional target-user assumption

The current **provisional, unvalidated** target user is a parent/caregiver responsible for setup around the first independently used smartphone transition, approximately the 10–12 life stage, initially oriented to the UK/England context.

L4 may design for this target as an owner-authorized assumption. It may not report that this cohort has demonstrated demand, usability, willingness, value, persistence, or preference.

## 5. Required provisional product outcome

L4 should define and design the smallest coherent UseSafeWeb experience that can later be tested and, only after the required downstream gates, implemented safely:

1. help the parent understand what protection is and is not being provided;
2. guide native safeguards first and avoid repeating safeguards already correctly configured;
3. guide activation and verification of the accepted encrypted AdGuard-backed baseline protection path;
4. support one genuinely relevant service safeguard when applicable rather than manufacturing work;
5. end in a truthful Protection Map that distinguishes system-verified, parent-confirmed, action-needed, not-covered, uncertain/error and removed states as applicable;
6. make recovery, removal, false-positive and unsupported/conflict paths understandable;
7. remain accountless-first, privacy-minimal, non-surveillance and self-service-oriented;
8. keep the public brand/website and setup/product experience coherent while preserving their distinct purposes.

These are **design outcomes to specify and test**, not claims that real users have achieved them.

## 10. Design decision rule under missing behavioral evidence

When L4 must choose among plausible alternatives without real-user evidence:

1. prefer the smallest reversible option consistent with owner-frozen scope and current technical reality;
2. minimize data, steps, accounts, choices, support burden and implementation complexity;
3. preserve truthful protection/uncertainty states rather than optimizing apparent completion;
4. label the choice `PROVISIONAL` when it materially depends on a user-behavior assumption;
5. record what real evidence would confirm/refute the assumption later;
6. do not create a large implementation commitment merely to compensate for missing validation.

## 11. Stop and revalidation conditions

Ordinary provisional L4 execution stops at the first applicable condition below:

- the selected task's own acceptance requires actual real-participant/user evidence that does not exist;
- a material product decision cannot be made conservatively/reversibly without pretending a customer preference or behavior is known;
- a HUMAN_ONLY or HUMAN_APPROVAL_REQUIRED action is reached without the required current authorization;
- a safety, security, privacy, technical or actual legal blocker applies to the proposed action;
- work would cross into integrated build/L5/L6, real participant processing, legal completion, payment activation or public launch without its own gate/authority;
- CR-0003 expires on **2027-08-27** or the Project Owner reactivates/supersedes L3 earlier;
- new real-user or other higher-quality evidence materially contradicts a provisional assumption.

At expiry/reactivation or on contradictory evidence, re-run the applicable deferred validation and **re-evaluate/reopen every downstream PASS materially dependent on the affected provisional assumption**. Do not protect a prior design decision from contrary evidence merely because work has already been done.
